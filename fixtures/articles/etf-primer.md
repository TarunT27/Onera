# Broad-market index ETFs: a short primer

*Synthetic research note written for the ClearFi demo. Not investment advice. Figures are illustrative.*

Index ETFs hold a basket of securities that tracks a published index. Investors usually compare them on three things:

1. **Expense ratio** — the annual fee as a share of assets. Broad US index funds commonly charge well under 0.10% a year.
2. **Tracking difference** — how closely the fund's return follows its index after costs.
3. **Exposure** — which companies, sectors and countries the index covers. A "total market" index is broader than a large-cap index.

Risks: index funds fall when their market falls; concentration in a few large companies can make a "diversified" index less diversified than it looks; international funds add currency risk.
